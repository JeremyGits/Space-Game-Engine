Overview

The post-processing system provides AAA-quality visual effects that transform your game from basic 3D rendering to professional, cinematic quality. All effects are GPU-accelerated and optimized for real-time performance.

🌟 What Post-Processing Adds

  • Bloom: Glowing lights and bright surfaces
  • SSAO: Realistic ambient shadows for depth
  • Chromatic Aberration: Cinematic color fringing
  • Vignette: Focus attention with darkened edges
  • Film Grain: Film-like texture and quality

Available Effects

🌟 Bloom

Creates a glow around bright areas like lights, emissive materials, and highlights. Essential for making lights feel bright and creating that "next-gen" look.

Parameters:
  • intensity: Glow strength (0.3 - 1.5)
  • luminanceThreshold: Brightness cutoff (0.75 - 0.95)
  • luminanceSmoothing: Transition smoothness (0.8 - 0.98)
  • kernelSize: Blur quality (SMALL to VERY_LARGE)
Visual Impact: High | Performance Cost: 2-3ms

🎭 SSAO (Screen Space Ambient Occlusion)

Adds realistic ambient shadows in crevices and corners. Makes geometry feel solid and grounded. Critical for depth perception and professional quality.

Parameters:
  • intensity: Shadow strength (0.5 - 1.5)
  • radius: Shadow spread (0.3 - 0.7)
  • samples: Quality (8, 16, 32, 64)
  • bias: Shadow offset (0.01 - 0.05)
Visual Impact: Very High | Performance Cost: 4-5ms

🌈 Chromatic Aberration

Simulates lens imperfections by slightly separating RGB channels. Adds a subtle cinematic quality and can enhance sci-fi aesthetics.

Parameters:
  • offset: RGB separation ([0.0005, 0.0005] - [0.003, 0.003])
  • radialModulation: Stronger at edges (true/false)
Visual Impact: Medium | Performance Cost: <1ms

📷 Vignette

Darkens the edges of the screen to focus attention on the center. Creates a cinematic framing effect.

Parameters:
  • darkness: Edge darkness (0.3 - 0.6)
  • offset: Vignette size (0.3 - 0.5)
Visual Impact: Medium | Performance Cost: <1ms

🎬 Film Grain

Adds subtle noise to simulate film texture. Gives a more organic, film-like quality to digital rendering.

Parameters:
  • opacity: Grain visibility (0.02 - 0.06)
Visual Impact: Low-Medium | Performance Cost: <1ms

Technical Details

Effect Pipeline Order

Scene Render → Bloom → SSAO → Chromatic Aberration → Vignette → Film Grain → Final Output

Shader Implementation

All effects use optimized fragment shaders running on the GPU. The EffectComposer manages the render pipeline and framebuffer management. 

// Internal pipeline (simplified):
1. Render scene to framebuffer
2. Extract bright areas (luminance threshold)
3. Apply gaussian blur (mipmap chain)
4. Calculate ambient occlusion (sample hemisphere)
5. Apply color fringing (RGB offset)
6. Darken edges (radial gradient)
7. Add noise texture (overlay blend)
8. Composite to screen
